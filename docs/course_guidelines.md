# Course rules (from Research_Proposal_Guidelines.pdf and syllabus)

## Proposal
- 3 pages max incl. references, IEEEtran conference template. Submit PDF + LaTeX source. Due Week 6. Individual.
- ≥ 5 peer-reviewed sources, IEEE style. Figures legible in grayscale; every figure/table referenced in text.
- Required: one testable RQ + hypothesis; related work ending in explicit gap; reproducible method; evaluation with credible baseline, ≥1 ablation/controlled comparison, ≥1 systems measure; risk register (technical, data, compute, ethical/security); reproducibility statement (repo, seeds, data versions, environment).
- AI Assistance Disclosure required (tool, purpose, what was verified/changed). Undisclosed AI content or fabricated references = academic-integrity violation. May be asked to explain orally.

## Rubric
Novelty & formulation 20% · Methodology 20% · Experimental design 25% · Analysis & systems insight 20% · Writing & disclosure 15%.

## Milestones
Proposal W6 · PR1 W10 · PR2 W13 · Final paper (6–8 pp IEEE/ACM) + artifact + defense/demo W16.

## Assessment weights (syllabus)
Assignments/labs 25% · Proposal 10% · Progress reports 25% · Final paper 25% + defense 15%.
